# Customer-Segment-Analysis
📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes an online retail dataset to understand customer purchasing behavior and business performance. Using Python (Pandas) for data preprocessing and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis, customers were segmented based on their purchasing patterns. An interactive Power BI dashboard was then developed to visualize key metrics
🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
Segment customers using the RFM model.
Identify high-value customers and at-risk customers.
Visualize sales trends and key business KPIs.
Support data-driven decision-making through interactive dashboards.

📈 RFM Analysis

Customers were segmented based on:

Recency – How recently a customer made a purchase.
Frequency – How often the customer purchases.
Monetary – How much the customer spends.

Customer segments include:

Champion
Loyal Customer
At Risk
New Customer
Regular Customer
💡 Key Business Insights
Champion customers contribute the largest share of total revenue.
Monthly revenue trends highlight seasonal sales patterns.
A small group of products generates a significant portion of total revenue.
Customer segmentation helps identify retention and marketing opportunities.
